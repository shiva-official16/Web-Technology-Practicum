      <ol>
        <li>Sensors collect raw data.</li>
        <li>Data is sent to the central server.</li>
        <li>Values are checked against safe limits.</li>
        <li>Results are published on this page.</li>

    <section id="positioning">
      <h2>CSS Positioning on This Page</h2>
      <p>A monitoring portal needs headers that stay visible, badges that sit on top of photos, pins placed
         on a scale and alerts that never scroll away. Each of those jobs uses a different
         <code>position</code> value. This section explains all five and shows where this page uses them.</p>

      <h3>The five values at a glance</h3>
      <div class="pos-grid">

        <!-- STATIC -->
        <article class="pos-card">
          <h4><code>static</code></h4>
          <div class="pos-demo">
            <div class="pos-block">Block 1</div>
            <div class="pos-block pos-target">Target (static)</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Block 3</div>
          </div>
          <p>The default. Elements follow the normal document flow, and <code>top</code>, <code>left</code>,
             <code>right</code> and <code>bottom</code> are ignored.</p>
          <p class="pos-use"><strong>On this page:</strong> paragraphs, lists and sections.</p>
        </article>

        <!-- RELATIVE -->
        <article class="pos-card">
          <h4><code>relative</code></h4>
          <div class="pos-demo">
            <div class="pos-block">Block 1</div>
            <div class="pos-block pos-target t-relative">Target (top: 8px; left: 24px)</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Block 3</div>
          </div>
          <p>The element is shifted from its normal spot, but its original space stays reserved, so Block 3
             does not move up. It also becomes the anchor for absolute children.</p>
          <p class="pos-use"><strong>On this page:</strong> image frames, the AQI scale, alert cards.</p>
        </article>

        <!-- ABSOLUTE -->
        <article class="pos-card">
          <h4><code>absolute</code></h4>
          <div class="pos-demo">
            <div class="pos-block">Block 1</div>
            <div class="pos-block pos-target t-absolute">Target (bottom: 8px; right: 8px)</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Block 3</div>
          </div>
          <p>The element leaves the flow (Block 3 moves up) and is placed against its nearest positioned
             ancestor, here the dashed box.</p>
          <p class="pos-use"><strong>On this page:</strong> photo tags, AQI pins, the "New" ribbon, the mobile menu dropdown.</p>
        </article>

        <!-- FIXED -->
        <article class="pos-card">
          <h4><code>fixed</code></h4>
          <div class="pos-demo pos-viewport" aria-hidden="true">
            <div class="scroll-track">
              <div class="pos-block">Page content</div>
              <div class="pos-block">Page content</div>
              <div class="pos-block">Page content</div>
              <div class="pos-block">Page content</div>
              <div class="pos-block">Page content</div>
              <div class="pos-block">Page content</div>
            </div>
            <span class="pos-block pos-target fixed-chip">Fixed</span>
          </div>
          <p>The element is placed against the browser window and stays put while the page scrolls
             (the demo above is a simulation).</p>
          <p class="pos-use"><strong>On this page:</strong> the live alert box (bottom left) and the
             "Back to top" button (bottom right).</p>
        </article>

        <!-- STICKY -->
        <article class="pos-card">
          <h4><code>sticky</code></h4>
          <div class="pos-demo pos-scroll" tabindex="0" role="region" aria-label="Scrollable sticky demo">
            <div class="sticky-head">Sticky heading (top: 0)</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 1</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 2</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 3</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 4</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 5</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 6</div>
            <div class="pos-block">Reading 7</div>
          </div>
          <p>Acts like <code>relative</code> until the scroll position reaches its <code>top</code> value, then
             it sticks. Scroll the box above to see it.</p>
          <p class="pos-use"><strong>On this page:</strong> the main navigation and the table header and
             first column.</p>
        </article>
      </div>
