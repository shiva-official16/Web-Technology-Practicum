        <li>Air quality (PM2.5, PM10, CO<sub>2</sub>)</li>

      <h3>How a reading is processed</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Sensors collect raw data.</li>
        <li>Data is sent to the central server.</li>
        <li>Values are checked against safe limits.</li>
        <li>Results are published on this page.</li>
      </ol>
      <h3>Key terms</h3>
      <dl>
        <div>
          <dt>AQI</dt>
          <dd>Air Quality Index, a single number that shows how clean or polluted the air is.</dd>
        </div>
        <div>
          <dt>pH</dt>
          <dd>A scale from 0 to 14 that shows how acidic or basic water is.</dd>
        </div>
      </dl>
