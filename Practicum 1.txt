      <h2>About Us</h2>
      <p>We are an environmental organization that tracks the health of the air we breathe and the water we use.
         This page shares our latest readings so that citizens and authorities can act early.</p>
      <p>Data is collected from sensor stations placed across the city and updated every hour.</p>

    <section id="indicators">
      <h2>Environmental Indicators</h2>
      <h3>What we measure</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Air quality (PM2.5, PM10, CO<sub>2</sub>)</li>
        <li>Water quality (pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen)</li>
        <li>Pollution (noise level, waste discharge)</li>
      </ul>
      <h3>How a reading is processed</h3>
      <ol>
        <li>Sensors collect raw data.</li>
        <li>Data is sent to the central server.</li>
        <li>Values are checked against safe limits.</li>
        <li>Results are published on this page.</li>
      </ol>
      <h3>Key terms</h3>
      <dl>
        <dt>AQI</dt>
        <dd>Air Quality Index, a single number that shows how clean or polluted the air is.</dd>
        <dt>pH</dt>
        <dd>A scale from 0 to 14 that shows how acidic or basic water is.</dd>
      </dl>
    </section>
